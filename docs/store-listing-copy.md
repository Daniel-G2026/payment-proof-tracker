### Full description
```
ProofLedger helps small businesses keep track of money in and money out — and keep the proof.

Record a payment in seconds: how much, the date, whether money came in or went out, and who paid you or who you paid. Attach the payment screenshot so the proof always stays with the record and you can find it later.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Money in / money out — log every payment with only the details that matter.
• Keep the proof — attach a payment screenshot to any record so the evidence is never lost.
• Who paid / who you paid — keep a simple list of the people and businesses you deal with.
• Projects & installments — set up a project (like a house or plot), record the agreed payment plan, and see at a glance who has paid, who's due, and who's behind.
• Reports & export — view your totals for the month or week and share a CSV or PDF.

WORKS OFFLINE
Your records are saved on your phone first, so the app works even with no signal. When you're back online, everything backs up safely so you can restore it on a new phone — proof images included.

SMART DRAFTS — YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Attach a payment screenshot and ProofLedger can suggest the amount, date, and other details to save you typing. You always review and confirm before anything is saved — nothing is recorded without you.

WHAT PROOFLEDGER IS NOT
ProofLedger is a record-keeping tool — not a bank or a payment app. It does not move money, and it is not full accounting, tax, or payroll software.

Your data is yours. We don't sell it and we don't show ads. You can export or delete your records at any time.
```
