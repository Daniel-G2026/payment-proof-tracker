## How to request deletion of your account and data

To delete your ProofLedger account and all associated data:

1. Email **proofledgerapp@yahoo.com**.
2. Use the subject line **"Delete my account"**.
3. Send it from — or include — the **email address you use to sign in to ProofLedger**, so we can verify the request is yours.

We will complete your request within **30 days**.

## What is deleted

We permanently delete all data tied to your account, including:
- Your account and sign-in details (name, email)
- Your business details
- All financial records (money in / money out, amounts, dates, references, categories, notes)
- All people you recorded (names, phone numbers, notes)
- All projects, units, and installment schedules
- All proof images you uploaded

Proof images and records are deleted within **30 days** of your request, including from backups.

## Deleting some of your data (without deleting your account)

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data:

- **In the app**, you can delete individual records, people, projects, or proof images at any time. Open the item and choose delete; it is removed from your account, and any associated proof image is deleted within 30 days, including from backups.
- **By email**, you can ask us to delete specific data — email **proofledgerapp@yahoo.com** with the subject **"Delete specific data"**, from the email address you sign in with, describing what you'd like removed. We action requests within 30 days.

Your account and all other data remain intact; only the items you choose are deleted.

## What is kept, and for how long

We do not retain your records after deletion, except where we are legally required to keep limited information for a short period (for example, to comply with the law or to prevent fraud/abuse); any such data is deleted once that purpose is met. Separately, raw AI-extraction data is automatically deleted after about 30 days as part of normal operation.

For more, see our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
